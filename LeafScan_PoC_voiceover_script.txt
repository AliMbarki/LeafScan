[0:00 - 0:08]  Title slide
Hello! We are the LeafScan team, and this is our proof of concept: an app that recognises plant diseases from a leaf.

[0:08 - 0:24]  Slide: The problem we address
Plant diseases destroy a big part of crops every year. The first signs show on the leaves, but recognising them needs an expert. Our app gives you the plant, the disease and advice in under a second, fully offline.

[0:24 - 0:42]  Slide: Technologies used
The interface is built with PySide6. OpenCV handles the camera, videos and finding the leaf. The AI model is MobileNetV2, run with ONNX Runtime. NumPy does the image maths, SQLite stores the history, Matplotlib draws the charts, and pytest runs sixty-four tests.

[0:42 - 0:58]  Slide: How a scan works
First we get the image, and OpenCV finds the leaf. The AI model identifies the plant and the disease, our rules check the confidence, and the result is shown and can be saved. Everything heavy runs in the background, so the window never freezes.

[0:58 - 1:03]  App: Scan page
This is the Scan page. We can use the webcam or import a photo.

[1:03 - 1:11]  App: tomato leaf imported
Here's a tomato leaf. The app outlines it and finds early blight, with over ninety-nine percent confidence.

[1:11 - 1:21]  App: advice card scrolling
Below is the advice card: the symptoms, the steps to take, how to prevent it, and our sources.

[1:21 - 1:27]  App: pink highlight off / on
The pink areas show the parts of the leaf that aren't a healthy green.

[1:27 - 1:31]  App: save scan
One click saves the scan to the history.

[1:31 - 1:45]  Slide: Step 1 - Finding the leaf
Finding the leaf isn't AI, it's image processing with OpenCV. On a plain background we use colours, on a busy one an algorithm called GrabCut, and as a last option, the biggest green area.

We didn't train a model from zero. MobileNetV2 was already trained on fifty-four thousand leaf photos: fourteen plants and thirty-eight classes. It needs about fifty milliseconds per image, and below sixty percent confidence, it says "not sure".

[3:14 - 3:32]  App: video playing with labelled leaves

[3:59 - 4:15]  Slide: Results
On clean lab photos, the app finds the right plant ninety-seven percent of the time, and the disease ninety-four percent. On real field photos it's weaker, because the model only learned from lab photos.

[4:15 - 4:23]  App: busy scene without a leaf

[4:23 - 4:38]  Slide: Limitations & next steps
The model only knows fourteen plants and was trained on lab photos. Next, we want to train it on real field photos, add more plants, and build a mobile version.
